NutriTrack: AI-powered nutritional analysis app

Final-year project, in progress

A mobile app that identifies packaged and unpackaged foods and shows calories, protein, fat and carbohydrates, with an AI assistant that answers questions about your own nutrition data.

  • LLM nutrition assistant on the Groq LLaMA 3 API, using tool-calling over the user's nutrition data, goals and food history.
  • CNN-based food recognition (Food-101, Indian food and custom datasets), barcode scanning and OCR for nutrition labels.
  • RESTful back-end services and a Supabase/PostgreSQL schema for authentication, sessions, food records and history.
  • TypeScript screens for the dashboard, food history, camera scanning and progress tracking.
  • AI features: health-condition food warnings, cooking-method nutrition adjuster, Indian food-swap suggester, weekly report card and family multi-profile mode.
